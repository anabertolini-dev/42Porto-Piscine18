	Write a program that takes a string as a parameter and prints
	its words sorted first by length and then alphabetically,
	with each word on a separate line.

	Alphabetical order must be case-insensitive. For example, "aA"
	and "Aa" are considered equal, and their original order must
	be preserved. Duplicate words must also be preserved.

	If the number of arguments is not 1, the program must print
	a newline.

	The string will contain only spaces, tabs, and alphanumeric
	characters.

	Words must be separated by exactly one space, with no spaces
	before the first word or after the last word on each line.

	Examples:
	$> ./ord_alphlong
	$
	$> ./ord_alphlong "De son baton il frappa la pierre et l eau jaillit" | cat -e
	l$
	De et il la$
	eau son$
	baton$
	frappa pierre$
	jaillit$
	$> ./ord_alphlong "A a b B cc ca cd" | cat -e
	A a b B$
	ca cc cd$
	$> ./ord_alphlong "Pour l Imperium de l humanite" | cat -e
	l l$
	de$
	Pour$
	humanite Imperium$
